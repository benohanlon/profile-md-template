# Rebuild this profile

These instructions are for the AI editing the repository.

1. Read [profile.md](profile.md). Its standalone italic paragraphs explain how to make each section your own. [benohanlon.md](https://benohanlon.md/) is a finished example of the profile and adventure.
2. Rebuild the profile around the pilot, the person whose CV or résumé the owner supplies. Replace the fictional identity, cases and starting choices with evidence from that CV and the supplied links or notes. Ask about gaps that matter and omit unverified claims. Confirm what the pilot wants public.
3. Keep the public **Agent instructions** and **Run the full OODA loop** headings. These guide visitors’ AIs through the adventure.
4. Once the profile is ready, remove the fictional-example notice and the standalone italic rebuild notes from the new copy. Run `node scripts/build.cjs` and `node scripts/build.cjs --check`. Preview `index.html` and `adventure.html`. Confirm the links and copy actions point to the new profile.
5. Show the owner the preview and ask before publishing their material.
