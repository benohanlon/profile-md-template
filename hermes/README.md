# CYOA Profile

Share your profile alongside your CV or in a follow-up. Send a prompt with your profile link so people can open their own AI chat, ask their own questions and get straight to the work that matters to them.

## Use it in Hermes

Enable **CYOA Profile** after installing it. In a fresh session, give Hermes your CV and work samples, then ask it to build a Choose Your Own Adventure `profile.md`. Review the draft for accuracy and decide what you want to share.

Send your profile with a prompt the reader can paste into their AI:

> Read [profile URL] and let me choose where to start.

They can ask a direct question or explore the starting choices. You can share the Markdown as an attachment or publish it with the [optional website starter](https://github.com/benohanlon/profile-md-template).

## Test a worked example

Open the [worked example](https://benohanlon.md/) and ask one question you would actually ask a prospective collaborator. Follow the answer to a case or example. Tell us what you asked, what you found and where you got stuck. [Give feedback](https://hiddenjobs.ai/profile-md/#feedback)

CYOA Profile was demoed at [AI Builders LCR](https://www.aibuilderslcr.co.uk/). [Make your own at Hidden Jobs](https://hiddenjobs.ai/profile-md/)

## What the skill uses

The package contains Markdown instructions and artwork. It registers no tools, hooks, middleware or MCP servers. It includes no executable code, plugin credentials, telemetry or background jobs. The skill reads CVs or work samples you explicitly share and can ask Hermes to fetch the public starter at `hiddenjobs.ai`, the worked example at `benohanlon.md` and sources you provide. Your configured AI provider processes the material. The optional website workflow can run the starter’s Node build and write preview files in your copy of the starter after you request that workflow. Publishing files or sending a prompt requires your approval.
