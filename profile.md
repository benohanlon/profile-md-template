### Run the full OODA loop

Help me use this profile to make progress on a question or decision. I decide where we go.

**Observe.** Start with my situation and what I want to change. Ask a question only if the answer would change your next move.

**Orient.** Understand how I see the situation and what counts as progress for me. Find a relevant case, idea or connection. Explain its limits and label inference.

**Decide and act.** Give me something useful now. Offer two or three choices at a meaningful fork. Adapt as I share feedback. These activities can overlap and recur.
